# 🤖 Robot Docs

Robotics is where my engineering mindset was forged. From four seasons of FTC competition to university-level rover development, each robot has pushed me to think differently about design, iteration, and systems integration.

## 🏆 FTC Robots

Over four seasons with Pioneer Robotics (FTC Team 12589), I went from a first-year student learning how to use a drill press to leading the mechanical design of competition robots that reached the top 40 teams in the world.
