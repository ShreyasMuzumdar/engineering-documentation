# 🤖 Robot Docs

Robotics is where my engineering mindset was forged. Over four seasons with Pioneer Robotics (FTC Team 12589), I went from a first-year student learning how to use a drill press to leading the mechanical design of competition robots that reached the top 40 teams in the world. Each season brought a new game, new constraints, and new problems I had never seen before. Each robot taught me something the previous one hadn't.

The through-line across all four seasons wasn't any single mechanism or material choice. It was the process: understand the game deeply, identify the highest-value actions, design around reliability first, and iterate fast enough to learn before competition day. The robots got more sophisticated each year, but the discipline behind them stayed the same.
